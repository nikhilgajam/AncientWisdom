📿 The 4 Vedas & 6 Vedāṅgas (చతుర్వేదాలు & షడ్వేదాంగాలు • चतुर्वेदाः षड्वेदाङ्गानि च)
Canonical Roots
🔥

1. Ṛgveda (ऋग्वेदः • ఋగ్వేదము)

10 Maṇḍalas • 1,028 Sūktas • Upaveda: Āyurveda

The oldest Vedic saṁhitā (~10,552 mantras) composed in sacred Chandas (Gāyatrī, Triṣṭubh, Jagatī), praising cosmic forces (Agni, Indra, Sūrya, Aśvinī Devatas). Presided over by the Hotā priest.

🪔

2. Yajurveda (यजुर्वेदः • యజుర్వేదము)

Śukla & Kṛṣṇa • Yajña Vidyā • Upaveda: Dhanurveda

The Veda of sacred rituals, prose mantras (Yajus), and altar geometry (Śulba Sūtras), preserved in Śukla (Vājasaneyi) and Kṛṣṇa (Taittirīya) recensions. Presided over by the Adhvaryu priest.

🎶

3. Sāmaveda (सामवेदः • సామవేదము)

1,875 Mantras • Svara Music • Upaveda: Gāndharvaveda

The Veda of sacred melody and harmonic chanting (Sāman), setting Ṛgvedic hymns to seven musical notes (Svaras)—the foundational root of Indian classical music. Presided over by the Udgātā priest.

🌿

4. Atharvaveda (अथर्ववेदः • అథర్వవేదము)

20 Kāṇḍas • ~5,987 Mantras • Upaveda: Arthaśāstra

The Veda of applied sciences, holistic healing, longevity, herbs, domestic harmony, and statecraft (including Prāṇa Sūkta and Bhūmi Sūkta). Presided over by the Brahmā priest.

🌬️

1. Śikṣā (शिक्षा • శిక్ష) — Nose / Breath

Phonetics, Articulation & Prāṇa • Pāṇinīya Śikṣā

Governs exact pronunciation, anatomical articulation places (Kaṇṭhya, Tālavya, Mūrdhanya, Dantya, Oṣṭhya), pitch accents (Udātta, Anudātta, Svarita), and breath regulation.

📐

2. Chandas (छन्दः • ఛందస్సు) — Feet

Prosody & Binary Combinatorics • Ācārya Piṅgala

Preserves Vedic verses through strict syllable counts and Laghu/Guru weights, giving rise to Prastāra (2n binary) and Meru Prastāra (Pascal's Triangle).

🗣️

3. Vyākaraṇa (व्याकरणम् • వ్యాకరణము) — Mouth

Generative Grammar • Pāṇini's Aṣṭādhyāyī (4,000 Sūtras)

The world's earliest formal generative grammar by Pāṇini (with Kātyāyana & Patañjali's Mahābhāṣya), encoding Sanskrit morphology in algebraic meta-rules (Māheśvara Sūtras).

📖

4. Nirukta (निरुक्तम् • నిరుక్తము) — Ears

Vedic Etymology & Semantics • Ācārya Yāska

Explains how Vedic words derive from verbal roots (Dhātus), commenting on the ancient Nighaṇṭu lexicon to preserve exact scriptural meaning across millennia.

🏛️

5. Kalpa (कल्पः • కల్పము) — Hands

Ritual, Dharma & Śulba Geometry • Baudhāyana

Encompasses Śrauta, Gṛhya, Dharma, and Śulba Sūtras—including the Pythagorean theorem, √2 approximations, and circle-squaring formulas used in Vedic altar construction.

🔭

6. Jyotiṣa (ज्योतिषम् • జ్యోతిషము) — Eyes

Astronomy, Timekeeping & Pañcāṅga • Lagadha & Varāhamihira

Tracks solar-lunar motions, solstices, eclipses, and the 27 Nakṣatras to fix sacred Yajña timings and civil calendars (Vedāṅga Jyotiṣa, Sūrya Siddhānta, Bṛhat Saṁhitā).
