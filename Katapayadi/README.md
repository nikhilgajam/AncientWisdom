# KaTaPaYadi Master (कटपयादि)
### Ancient Indian Alphanumeric Cipher & Mnemonic Studio

> **"कादिर्नव टादिर्नव पादिपञ्चक यद्यष्टकम्। ज्ञेया शून्या स्वरास्तद्वत् अक्षरेषु स्वरान्तिकाः॥"**  
> *"Ka begins nine (1-9), Ṭa begins nine (1-9), Pa begins five (1-5), Ya begins eight (1-8). Standalone vowels and ña/na represent Zero. In conjunct consonants, only the last consonant counts."*  
> — **अङ्कानां वामतो गतिः** *(Numbers proceed from right to left)*

---

## 🌟 Overview
**KaTaPaYadi Master** is an interactive, responsive web application and memory studio built with **HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript**. It is designed to teach, decode, encode, and help you remember any numeric information (passwords, PINs, birthdays, math constants) using the ancient Indian Katapayadi cipher system.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. 📜 Master Matrix & Interactive Rule Guide
- **Dynamic 10-Column Matrix**: Categorized across the 4 primary series (**Ka**, **Ṭa**, **Pa**, **Ya**) and vowels.
- **Multilingual Script Switcher**: Toggle seamlessly between **देवनागरी (Devanagari)**, **తెలుగు (Telugu)**, **English (IAST / Phonetic)**, or **All Scripts Combined**.
- **Interactive Letter Inspection**: Click any cell to inspect its column, phonetic hints, listen to studio audio, and insert either the Devanagari or Telugu letter directly into the converter.
- **The 4 Core Rules**: Clear visual cards explaining consonant roots, vowel neutrality, conjunct rules (samyuktaksharas / వత్తులు), and the principle of *Aṅkānāṁ Vāmato Gatiḥ* (*అంకానాం వామతో గతిః*).

### 2. 🗣️ Sanskrit & Telugu Varṇamālā & Pronunciation Guide (వర్ణమాల & ధ్వనిశాస్త్రం)
- **All 52 Aksharas**: Comprehensive coverage in both Devanagari and Telugu of all 16 Vowels (स्वराः / అచ్చులు), 25 Classified Consonants (स्पर्श / స్పర్శాలు), 4 Semi-vowels (अन्तःस्थाः / అంతస్థాలు), 4 Sibilants/Aspirate (ऊष्माणः / ఊష్మాలు), and Special Conjuncts (क्ष/క్ష, ज्ञ/జ్ఞ, ळ/ళ).
- **Anatomical Places of Articulation (स्थान)**: Kaṇṭhya (Velar/Throat), Tālavya (Palatal), Mūrdhanya (Retroflex), Dantya (Dental), and Oṣṭhya (Labial).
- **Effort Classification (प्रयत्न)**: Alpa-prāṇa (Unaspirated), Mahā-prāṇa (Aspirated), Aghoṣa (Voiceless), Ghoṣa (Voiced), and Anunāsika (Nasal).
- **Studio Audio Pronunciation**: Built-in audio playback for each letter and sequential row audition downloaded from *oursanskrit.com*.
- **Live Search & Filter**: Search in Devanagari, Telugu, IPA, or English IAST.

### 3. 🔄 Two-Way Converter (Encode & Decode)
- **Text to Number (Decode)**:
  - Input Devanagari Sanskrit, Telugu script, or phonetic English (e.g. `గోపీభాగ్యమధువ్రాతః`, `आయురారోగ్యసౌఖ్యము`, `गोपीभाग्यमधुव्रातः`, `माया`, `kanakangi`).
  - Letter-by-letter chip decomposition showing exact syllable tokenization and last-consonant isolation (including Telugu conjuncts / వత్తులు).
  - One-click toggle for **Vāmato Gatiḥ** (Right-to-Left classical order).
  - Comprehensive Virtual Katapayadi Keyboard with **Devanagari $\leftrightarrow$ Telugu** live switch, containing ALL 35 consonants with numeric badges, all 16 standalone vowels, and all 18 mātrās & diacritic signs (halant, anusvara, visarga).
- **Number to Text (Encode / Mnemonic Composer)**:
  - Enter any number (e.g. `314159`, `2026`, `1947`, `4028`).
  - Interactive consonant picker per digit.
  - Vowel modifier bar (mātrās: `a`, `ā`, `i`, `ī`, `u`, `e`, `o`, etc.) to craft natural words.
  - Smart dictionary match suggestions from classical Sanskrit roots and words.

### 3. 🧠 "Remember Anything" Memory Studio (Personal Vault)
- **Mnemonic Architect**:
  - Presets for **ATM / Secret PINs**, **Important Dates**, **Phone Numbers**, and **Science Constants**.
  - Generates syllable suggestions for your exact digits.
  - Allows you to write vivid visual imagery stories (the Roman Room / Loci memory technique).
- **Persistent Memory Vault**:
  - Automatically saves your custom memory cards in browser `localStorage`.
  - Filter and search through your saved mnemonics.
  - Single-click to inspect and decode any saved card back in the converter.
  - Export and Import your memory vault as JSON files for backup across devices.

### 4. ✨ Historic Masterpieces Decoded
- **The Epic Cryptogram: "Jaya" (जय / జయ) = 18 in the Mahābhārata**:
  - The sacred invocation *ततो जयमुदीरयेत् (tato Jayam udīrayet)* reveals the original title of the epic: **Jaya**.
  - Decoded via Katapayadi: **ज / జ (Ja) = 8**, **य / య (Ya) = 1**.
  - Applying *Aṅkānāṃ Vāmato Gatiḥ* (reading backwards): **18**!
  - Unveils the sacred 18-fold matrix of the epic: **18 Parvas (Books)**, **18 Days of the Kurukshetra War**, **18 Akṣauhiṇī armies**, **18 Chapters of the Bhagavad Gītā**, and **18 surviving warriors**.
- **The Divine π (Pi) Shloka**:
  - *गोपीभाग्यमधुव्रातः श्रृङ्गोदधिसन्धगः । खलजीवितखाताव गलहालारसंधरः ॥*
  - Complete 32-syllable interactive visualizer encoding π / 10 = 0.31415926535897932384626433832795...
  - Syllable-by-syllable animated audio playback.
- **Narayaneeyam Kali Day Chronogram**:
  - Explains Melpathur Narayana Bhattathiri's concluding chronogram *आयुरा॑रोग्यसौख्यम्* encoding Kali Day `1,712,210` (December 13, 1586 CE).
- **Madhava's Sine Tables & Candravākyas**:
  - Trigonometric tables encoded in Sanskrit poetry centuries before calculus in Europe.

### 5. 📜 Chandas (छन्दस् - Prosody) & Mathematical Interlock
- **Pingala's Binary Metric System**:
  - Explores how Acharya Pingala's *Chandaḥ-śāstra* (c. 3rd-2nd century BCE) pioneered the binary number system through metric syllables: **Laghu (| = 1 matra, light)** and **Guru (ऽ = 2 matras, heavy)**.
  - The classic mnemonic sutra: **यमाताराजभानसलगाः** (*ya-mā-tā-rā-ja-bhā-na-sa-la-gāḥ*), generating the 8 cyclic Ashta Ganas ($2^3 = 8$).
- **Interactive Metre Scanner & Gana Analyzer**:
  - Live syllable syllabification testing short vowels, long vowels, conjunct-preceding syllables (saṁyoga-paraḥ), and visarga/anusvara.
  - Automatic detection of standard Vedic and Classical metres (Anuṣṭubh, Gāyatrī, Indravajrā, Upendravajrā, Śārdūlavikrīḍita, Vasantatilakā, etc.).
  - Side-by-side display of the **Metric Matrix (Laghu/Guru weights)** and the **KaTaPaYadi Number Payload**.
- **Dual-Layer Information Architecture**:
  - Explains the profound mathematical relationship where **Chandas functions as an error-detecting checksum** (verifying rhythm and syllable count across oral transmission) while **KaTaPaYadi serves as the payload cipher** (transmitting mathematical and astronomical data without perturbing the poetic metre).
  - Demonstrates *Vāmato Gatiḥ* right-to-left permutation correspondence between Pingala's Prastāra combinatorics and Katapayadi's numerical reading.
