# Bṛhat Saṁhitā (బృహత్సంహిత) — Varāhamihira

Complete bilingual (**Dual / తెలుగు / English**) interactive web laboratory for **Varāhamihira's *Bṛhat Saṁhitā*** (based on V. Subrahmanya Sastri & M. Ramakrishna Bhat's 107-Adhyāya edition).

## Interactive Modules & Calculators
1. **Foundations, Planetary Courses & Sky Phenomena (`Ch. I–XIII`)**: 3 Skandhas, 5 Siddhāntas, Eclipse astronomy (`Ch. V`), 7 Planetary transits, Comets (`Ch. XI`), Agastya (`Ch. XII`), and Saptarṣis (`Ch. XIII`).
2. **Kūrmavibhāga 9-Region Globe (`Ch. XIV–XX`)**: Clickable 3×3 directional compass mapping all 9 Nakṣatra triads to regions of India/Earth, plus Planetary Wars (`Ch. XVII`) & Year Lords (`Ch. XIX`).
3. **Meteorology & Rainfall (`Ch. XXI–XXXI`)**: **195-Day Cloud Delivery Calculator** (`Ch. XXI`) and **Seasonal Droṇa Rainfall Selector** (`Ch. XXIII`).
4. **Earthquakes, Meteors, Crops & Prices (`Ch. XXXII–LII`)**: All 4 Earthquake Circles (Wind, Fire, Indra, Varuṇa), atmospheric optics, crop yogas, price schedules, and Puṣyasnāna.
5. **Interactive 81-Pada Vāstu Maṇḍala (`Ch. LIII`)**: Clickable `9×9` 45-deity Vāstu Puruṣa grid, Rank/Varṇa house dimensions calculator (`Sl. 4–19`), and classical house plans.
6. **Dakārgala Water Springs, Trees, Temples & Idols (`Ch. LIV–LXVII`)**: 8-direction Well-Placement Compass, Vṛkṣāyurveda, 20 Temple types, Vajralepa, 108-digit Tālamāna, and animal signs.
7. **Pañca Mahāpuruṣa, Gandhayukti & Gemology (`Ch. LXVIII–LXXXV`)**: 5 Great Men explorer, **Gandhārṇava Perfume Combinatorics Calculator** (`C(16,4)×96 = 174,720`), and Ratnaparīkṣā.
8. **Śākuna 8-Watch Omen Checker (`Ch. LXXXVI–XCVI`)**: Interactive 3-hour watch × 8-direction checker (`Aṅgāra, Dīpta, Dhūminī, Śānta`).
9. **Gocara Transit Checker & Muhūrta (`Ch. XCVII–CV`)**: Live Moon-sign to Transit-sign benefic/malefic checker (`Ch. CIV, Sl. 4`).
10. **Searchable 107-Adhyāya Book Index (`Ch. CVI–CVII`)** & **12-Question Mastery Quiz**.
