🌱 1. Lightning Mental Shortcuts & Arithmetic Proofs

Master the foundational mental math pillars: squaring numbers ending in 5, multiplying by 9s instantly, and validating every calculation in 1 second using the ancient Bījaṅka (Digital Root) test of 9.

1.1 Ekādhikena Pūrveṇa (एकाधिकेन पूर्वेण • ఏకాధికేన पूर्వేణ)

"By one more than the previous one" — Instant mental squaring of any number ending in 5 ($N = a5 \rightarrow [a \times (a+1)] \mid 25$).

Calculated Square:
--
Mental shortcut: $75^2 = [7 \times 8] \mid 25 = 56 \mid 25 = 5,625$.

1.2 Ekanyūnena Pūrveṇa (एकन्यूनेन पूर्वेण • ఏకన్యూనేన पूर्वेణ) — Instant Multiplication by 9, 99, 999...

"By one less than the previous one" — Effortlessly multiplies any number by repeated 9s across all three cases: equal digits, more 9s, or fewer 9s.

Instant Product:
--
Notice how $654 \times 999$ becomes $(654 - 1) = 653$ and $(999 - 653) = 346 \rightarrow 653,346$ in one mental breath!

1.3 Bījaṅka Checksum Engine (बीजाङ्क • బీజాంకము / नवांशावशेष • నవాంశావశేషము) — The Ancient Test of Nine

Ancient Vedic mathematicians never submitted a computation without checking its Bījaṅka (digital root modulo 9). Test addition, subtraction, or multiplication instantly!

Arithmetic Operation:
--
The digital root invariance theorem: $\text{DR}(A \times B) \equiv \text{DR}(A) \times \text{DR}(B) \pmod 9$.

⚡ 2. Universal Multiplication, Duplex Squaring & Vinculum

Moving beyond special cases to universal arithmetic algorithms that work for numbers and polynomials of any length.

2.1 Ūrdhva-Tiryagbhyām (ऊर्ध्वतिर्यग्भ्याम् • ఊర్ధ్వతిర్యగ్భ్యామ్) — "Vertically and Crosswise"

The universal multiplication algorithm. Computes cross-product rays from right to left in discrete column passes.

Final Product:
--
Single-pass cross multiplication without writing down intermediate multi-line work.

2.2 Nikhilaṁ Navataścaramaṁ Daśataḥ (निखिलं नवतश्चरमं दशतः)

"All from 9 and last from 10" — Effortless base multiplication using deviations from powers of 10.

Product via Deficiency:
--
Cross-addition of opposite deviation gives left side; multiplying deviations gives right side.

2.3 Dvandva Yoga (द्वन्द्वयोगः • ద్వంద్వయోగము) — The Universal Duplex Squaring Method

The fastest algorithm in mathematical history for squaring any arbitrary number (2-digit, 3-digit, 4-digit, etc.) without full cross-multiplication: $D(a) = a^2$, $D(ab) = 2ab$, $D(abc) = 2ac + b^2$, $D(abcd) = 2ad + 2bc$.

Duplex Square (D-Yoga):
--
Notice how $345^2$ decomposes into $D(3) \mid D(34) \mid D(345) \mid D(45) \mid D(5) = 9 \mid 24 \mid 46 \mid 40 \mid 25 \rightarrow 119,025$.

2.4 Yāvadūnam (यावदूनम् • యావదూనము) — Near-Base Squaring

"Whatever the extent of its deficiency, lessen it further and square the deficiency" ($(B \pm d)^2 = (B \pm 2d) \mid d^2$).

Near-Base Square:
--
$96^2 = (96 - 4) \mid 4^2 = 92 \mid 16 = 9,216$ in 1 second!

2.5 Vinculum Algebra (रेखाङ्क • రేఖాంకము / Bar Numbers)

Converts large friction digits (6, 7, 8, 9) into negative complements $\le 5$ with bar notation ($\bar{d}$), simplifying difficult multiplications into effortless mental additions.

🏹 3. Dhvajāṅka (The Flag Method) Universal Single-Line Division

Considered the crown jewel of Vedic arithmetic. While conventional long division requires an entire sheet of paper, Dhvajāṅka yields the exact quotient and remainder for ANY divisor in a single oral line!

Dhvajāṅka (ध्वजाङ्क • ధ్వజాంకము) Interactive Division Studio

Split the divisor into an Operator Digit (which divides) and a Flag Digit (which adjusts).

Quotient & Remainder:
--
Single-line Vedic division without tedious multiple trial subtractions.

👑 4. Vilokanam (By Inspection) Instant Roots

Extract cube roots of exact powers in under 2 seconds by pure observation of endings and bounds.

Vilokanam (विलोकनम् • విలోకనము) — Instant Cube Root ($\sqrt[3]{N}$) Extractor

Because digits 1, 4, 5, 6, 9, 0 retain their identity when cubed, while 2 ↔ 8 and 3 ↔ 7 form complementary pairs, any cube up to 1,000,000 resolves instantly into its tens and units digits!

Cube Root by Inspection:
--
$175,616 \rightarrow$ Last three $616$ ends in $6 \Rightarrow$ units digit is $6$. Leading $175 \Rightarrow 5^3=125 \le 175 < 6^3=216 \Rightarrow$ tens digit is $5$. Root = $56$!
