## 🌟 Overview

**Sūrya Siddhānta** is the foundational canonical treatise of classical Indian mathematical astronomy (*Siddhānta Jyotiṣa*). Built with zero-dependency HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript, this interactive studio covers **all 14 Adhyāyas (Chapters)** across **20 interactive sections**, with:

- **Bilingual Support (`Dual` / `తెలుగు` / `English`)**: Full Telugu (*Noto Sans Telugu*) and English coverage across chapter titles, 27 Nakshatras, 12 Rāśis, Birth Star results, and section guides.
- **Built-in Chapter Reader's Guides**: Every technical section includes a *"What This Is & How to Use (As Per Book)"* card explaining classical terms and step-by-step usage.
- **Progressive Learning Tiers**:
  - **Beginner**: Cosmic time scales, spherical Earth (*Bhūgola*), 27/28 Nakshatras & Birth Star finder, and the 9 time systems (*Nava Mānāni*).
  - **Intermediate**: Continuous *Ahargaṇa*, Time Lords (*Horā* / 60-year Jovian cycle), 24-Jyā Sine interpolation, 12-aṅgula *Śaṅku* gnomon, *Matsya* surveying, Lunar Eclipses, Conjunctions, Heliacal visibility, Moon Cusp elevation (*Śṛṅgonnati*), and *Mahāpāta*.
  - **Astronomer / Pro**: 4-Step *Spaṣṭīkaraṇa* planetary solver with *Śīghra-karṇa* hypotenuse & double-epicycle retrograde simulator, Solar Eclipse parallax (*Lambana* & *Nati*), and Geometric *Chedyaka* projection (*Valana*).

## 🚀 All 20 Interactive Sections (Mapped to the 14 Adhyāyas)

1. **Cosmic Time, Spherical Earth (*Bhūgola*) & *Ahargaṇa*** *(Ch. 1 & 12)* — Mahāyuga (`4,320,000` solar years), `21,600` Prāṇas/day, Earth diameter (`1,600` Yojanas), and intrinsic gravity (*Dhāraṇātmikā Śaktiḥ*).
2. **Live *Ahargaṇa* & Mean Planetary Longitude Calculator** *(Ch. 1)* — Computes elapsed civil days since the Kaliyuga epoch (Feb 17/18, 3102 BCE; JD `588465.5`) and mean positions for all **9 bodies**: Sun, Moon, Moon Apogee (*Mandocca*), Rāhu (retrograde node), Mars, Mercury *Śīghra*, Jupiter, Venus *Śīghra*, and Saturn.
3. **Time Lords (*Varṣeśa, Māseśa, Dineśa, Horā*) & 60-Year Jovian Cycle** *(Ch. 1 & 14)* — Planetary rulers for year, month, weekday, hourly *Horā*, and the 60 *Bārhaspatya Samvatsaras*.
4. **Trigonometry: 24-Jyā (Sine) Table & Arbitrary Angle Converter** *(Ch. 2, vv. 15–27)* — Radius `R = 3438'` with `225'` (`3° 45'`) steps, second-order finite difference formula `J(n+1) - J(n) = [J(n) - J(n-1)] - J(n)/225`, interactive 24-card grid, and live step-by-step *Jyā*, *Koṭijyā*, and *Utkramajyā* interpolation.
5. **True Planetary Places: 4-Step *Spaṣṭīkaraṇa* Solver & Double Epicycle Simulator** *(Ch. 2, vv. 34–45)* — Canonical 4-step *Manda + Śīghra* algorithm for the 5 *Tārāgrahas* (Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn) using odd/even quadrant epicycle interpolation and the *Śīghra-karṇa* hypotenuse formula (`atan2(r sin α, R + r cos α)`), paired with a color-coded HTML5 Canvas epicycle & retrograde (*Vakragati*) simulator.
6. **12-Aṅgula *Śaṅku* (Gnomon) & Latitude Lab** *(Ch. 3)* — Computes noon shadow length `s = 12 cot(α)`, hypotenuse *Karṇa*, equinoctial shadow *Palabhā* (`tan φ = s₀ / 12`), and *Cara* day-length variation.
7. ***Matsya* (Fish) Cardinal Surveying & *Deśāntara* Correction** *(Ch. 3 & 1)* — Interactive morning/evening shadow circle for True North–South alignment and Ujjain (`75°46' E`) Prime Meridian longitude/time offset.
8. **Lunar Eclipses & Earth's Shadow Cone (*Bhūchāyā / Tamomāna*)** *(Ch. 4)* — Shadow cone geometry (`~80'` width at Moon's distance), *Grāsa* (magnitude in digits), and *Sthiti* half-duration.
9. **Solar Eclipse Parallax: *Lambana* & *Nati*** *(Ch. 5)* — Topocentric longitude parallax (*Lambana* in time) and latitude parallax (*Nati* in arcminutes) explaining city-by-city solar eclipse variation.
10. **Eclipse Geometric Projection (*Chedyaka*) & *Valana*** *(Ch. 6)* — Visualizes *Sparśa* (first contact), *Madhya*, and *Mokṣa* (release) deflected by *Akṣa-valana* and *Ayana-valana*.
11. **Planetary Conjunctions (*Yuti*) & *Graha-Yuddha*** *(Ch. 7)* — Classifies close planetary encounters into *Bheda*, *Ullekha*, *Aṁśuvimarda*, and *Apasavya*.
12. **28 Nakshatras, *Yogatārā* Catalog & Personal *Janma Nakṣatra* Finder** *(Ch. 8)* — Full *Dhruvaka/Vikṣepa* catalog, daily Moon transit inspector, 7-day forecast, and **Drik-Gaṇita (*Bīja*-corrected + Chitrapakṣa/Lahiri Ayanāṁśa)** Birth Star calculator with Rāśi, Pāda, Vedic naming sound (*Nāmākṣara*), and bilingual Telugu/English astrological profiles.
13. **Heliacal Risings & Settings (*Udayāsta* / *Kālāṁśa*)** *(Ch. 9)* — Combustion limits near the Sun (Moon `12°`, Mars `17°`, Mercury `14°/12°`, Jupiter `11°`, Venus `10°/8°`, Saturn `15°`).
14. **Moon Phase & Elevation of Cusps (*Śṛṅgonnati*)** *(Ch. 10)* — Illuminated digits (*Śukla* in 12 Aṅgulas) and dynamic crescent horn tilt canvas.
15. **Malignant Aspects: *Vyatīpāta* & *Vaidhṛti* (*Mahāpāta*)** *(Ch. 11)* — Equal Sun/Moon declination (*Krānti-sāmya*) detector.
16. **Cosmography: *Bhūgola*, 4 Equatorial Cities & Mount Meru** *(Ch. 12)* — *Laṅkā*, *Yamakoṭi*, *Siddhāpura*, *Romaka* (`90°` apart), and polar day/night geometry.
17. **Astronomical Instruments (*Yantrādhyāya*)** *(Ch. 13)* — *Gola-Yantra* (Armillary Sphere), *Ghaṭī-Yantra* (24-minute copper water clock), and *Svayaṁvaha* wheel.
18. **The Nine Modes of Time (*Nava Mānāni*)** *(Ch. 14)* — *Sāvana, Saura, Cāndra, Nākṣatra, Bārhaspatya, Paitrya, Daiva, Prājāpatya,* and *Brāhma*.
19. **14 *Adhyāyas* Syllabus & Burgess Reader** — Interactive chapter browser with Sanskrit verses, Burgess translations, math formulas, and Telugu chapter titles.
20. **Mastery Assessment Quiz** — 10-question interactive quiz spanning all 14 chapters with explanations.
