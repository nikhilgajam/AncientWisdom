## 🌟 The Five Limbs of Sacred Time (పञ्च అంగాలు)

1. **Tithi (तिथि • తిథి)**:
   - Lunar day calculated from Moon-Sun elongation:
     $$\text{Tithi} = \left\lfloor \frac{(\lambda_{moon} - \lambda_{sun}) \bmod 360^\circ}{12^\circ} \right\rfloor + 1$$
   - 30 Tithis across *Śukla Pakṣa* (Bright fortnight) and *Kṛṣṇa Pakṣa* (Dark fortnight).
2. **Vāra (वार • వారము)**:
   - Solar day ruled by the planetary lord of the day's first Horā (Sūrya, Candra, Maṅgala, Budha, Guru, Śukra, Śani).
3. **Nakṣatra (नक्षत्र • నక్షత్రము)**:
   - Active lunar mansion based on the Moon's sidereal longitude ($\lambda_{moon} / 13^\circ 20'$), with Pāda 1–4 ($3^\circ 20'$ each).
4. **Yoga (योग • యోగము)**:
   - Luni-solar angular sum:
     $$\text{Yoga} = \left\lfloor \frac{(\lambda_{sun} + \lambda_{moon}) \bmod 360^\circ}{13^\circ 20'} \right\rfloor + 1$$
   - 27 canonical Yogas from Viṣkambha to Vaidhṛti.
5. **Karaṇa (करण • కరణము)**:
   - Half-tithi interval ($6^\circ$). 7 movable (*Carā*) and 4 fixed (*Sthirā*) karaṇas (60 per lunar month).

## 🪐 The 60-Year Jovian Cycle (Bṛhaspati Samvatsara • బృహస్పతి సంవత్సర చక్రము)
Organized by Jupiter's transit through the zodiac (12 years $\times 5$ elements = 60 years):
- 2024–2025: **Krodhin (क्रोधी • క్రోధి)**
- 2025–2026: **Viśvāvasu (विश्वावसु • విశ్వావసు)**
- 2026–2027: **Parābhava (पराभव • పరాభవ)**

## ⏱️ Precision Timing (Muhūrta)
- **Abhijit Muhūrta (అభిజిత్ ముహూర్తము)**: The supreme 8th daytime victory window.
- **Brāhma Muhūrta (బ్రాహ్మీ ముహూర్తము)**: 96 minutes before sunrise for meditation and study.
- **Rāhu Kāla (రాహుకాలము)**: 90-minute daily inauspicious window.
