## 🏛️ Executive Summary

**Vāstu-śāstra** is the ancient Indian architectural science governing spatial orientation, elemental balance (*Pañca-Mahābhūtas*), solar illumination cycles, magnetic alignments, and topographic gravity gradients.

This digital laboratory provides an **exact, non-superstitious, computationally verified realization** of Sri Gouru Thirupati Reddy's complete 352-page work (8th Edition, Prajahitha Publishers, 2013). By stripping away superstitious hearsay and astrological confusion, Thirupati Reddy grounded Vaastu on empirical geometry, the 90° right-angled perimeter (*Gunakam* / మూలమట్టం), and the immutable physics of environmental energy fields.

## 🌟 Comprehensive 10-Module System

### 1. “Vaasthu at a Glance” — The 16-Zone Perimeter Matrix (Pages 12–13)
Every rectangular plot and residential boundary is divided into 16 distinct perimeter sectors:
- **4 Cardinal Directions** bisected into **Exalted (Uccha • ఉచ్ఛ)** and **Exiguous / Debilitated (Neecha • నీచ)** sectors:
  - **East**: Eastern Northeast (ENE - Uccha, Auspicious for Gate, Water Sump, Borewell) vs. Eastern Southeast (ESE - Neecha, Litigation, Fire, Female illness).
  - **South**: Southern Southeast (SSE - Uccha, Kitchen hearth, secondary exit) vs. Southern Southwest (SSW - Neecha, Fatal "cobra bite", sudden ruin).
  - **West**: Western Southwest (WSW - Uccha, Master bedroom, heavy masonry, overhead tank) vs. Western Northwest (WNW - Neecha, Instability, loss of peace).
  - **North**: Northern Northwest (NNW - Neecha, Enemies, disputes, acceptable only for detached septic tank) vs. Northern Northeast (NNE - Uccha, Kubera's seat, boundless wealth, borewell).
- **4 Corner Diagonals (The SAFEW Elemental Matrix)**:
  - **Eshanya (ఈశాన్యం • NE)**: Water (*Jala*). Ruled by Ishana / Lord Shiva. Lowest elevation, maximum open setback, pristine water sources, puja room. Strictly zero toilets, zero kitchens, zero heavy stairs.
  - **Agneya (ఆగ్నేయం • SE)**: Fire (*Agni*). Ruled by Agni Deva. Kitchen cooking hearth (facing East), transformers, generators, inverters. Never place underground water sumps here.
  - **Niruthi (నైరుతి • SW)**: Earth (*Bhoomi*). Ruled by Nirriti / Pitrus. Highest elevation, thickest compound walls, master bedroom, overhead tank. Always commence foundation laying from this corner.
  - **Vayavya (వాయువ్యం • NW)**: Air (*Vayu*). Ruled by Vayu Deva. Guest bedroom, unmarried daughters' room, finished goods dispatch, detached septic tank in NNW.
- **Brahmasthana (బ్రహ్మస్థానము • Cosmic Center)**:
  - Space / Ether (*Akasha*). Navel of the dwelling. Kept open, well-lit, unencumbered by pillars, load-bearing crossbeams, or water pits.

### 2. Street Focus & Road Thrust Simulator (Vīthi Śūla • వీధి శూల — Page 76)
Simulates dynamic kinetic pressure when roads terminate directly into a plot boundary:
- **Beneficent (శుభ వీధి పోటులు)**:
  1. East of Northeast (ENE) — Fame, prosperity, intellectual elevation.
  2. North of Northeast (NNE) — Boundless wealth (Kubera blessing), peace of mind.
  3. South of Southeast (SSE) — Health and vitality for homemakers, business drive.
  4. West of Northwest (WNW) — Dynamic commercial turnover and societal respect for men.
- **Baneful / Detrimental (అశుభ వీధి పోటులు)**:
  1. East of Southeast (ESE) — Fire hazards, gynecological disorders, theft, litigation.
  2. South of Southwest (SSW) — Sri Thirupati Reddy's famous warning: *"Southern southwest gate is black cobra venomous bite."* Fatal downfalls, suicidal depression, insolvency.
  3. West of Southwest (WSW) — Moral degradation, persistent lawsuits, partnership collapses.
  4. North of Northwest (NNW) — Hostile enemies, restless wandering, unprovoked lawsuits.
- **Thirupati Reddy's Architectural Remedies**:
  - Erecting solid, elevated screening walls.
  - Positioning the main gate strictly within the Exalted sub-zone.
  - Creating interior buffer zones or planting dense Ashoka trees to deflect vehicular kinetic glare.

### 3. Angular Plots & The Set-Square Principle (Gunakam / గుణకం — Page 299)
- All dwellings must possess true 90° right angles.
- **The Only Permissible Extension**: An extension in the Northeast corner (ఈశాన్యం పెరగడం) multiplies wealth and spiritual grace.
- **Defective Extensions**: Extended Southeast (*Agni Vikriti*), extended Southwest (fatal ruin), or extended Northwest (restlessness).
- **The Gunakam Solution**: Demarcate a perfect 90° rectangular compound using a set-square, leaving the irregular triangular sliver outside the living perimeter as an external garden or selling it off.

### 4. Site Classification & 4 Grades of Building Sites (Pages 61–63)
- **First Grade Site (ప్రథమ శ్రేణి స్థలము)**: Roads on all four sides with East/North lower than South/West; or roads on East, North, and West with higher West road; or protruding into Northern/Eastern Northeast. High prosperity (*Deva-sthana*).
- **Second Grade Site (ద్వితీయ శ్రేణి స్థలము)**: Roads in East & West (East lower) or North & South (North lower). Steady growth.
- **Third Grade Site (తృతీయ శ్రేణి స్థలము)**: Single West road or East road joined with lower South road. Requires specific earth-filling and SW masonry.
- **Fourth Grade Site (చతుర్థ శ్రేణి స్థలము)**: West & South roads lower than the site with higher East/North roads. Requires extensive rectification before construction.
