🌌 1. Pañca Mahābhūta (पञ्चमहाभूतानि • పంచమహాభూతాలు)

The Five Primordial Elements from which all matter, tissues, and biological energies in the cosmos condense.

Space / Ether

Ākāśa (आकाश • ఆకాశము)

Non-resistance, omnipresence, sound (Śabda • శబ్దము). Manifests as internal bodily cavities: mouth, nostrils, thorax, abdomen.

Air / Motion

Vāyu (वायु • వాయువు)

Kinetic force, touch (Sparśa • స్పర్శ). Manifests as nerve impulses, cardiac contractions, peristalsis, and joint mobility.

Fire / Transformation

Agni / Tejas (अग्नि/तेजस् • అగ్ని/తేజస్సు)

Light, form, vision (Rūpa • రూపము). Manifests as enzymatic digestion, metabolic transformation, body temperature, and intellect.

Water / Cohesion

Jala / Āpas (जल/आपस् • జలము)

Liquidity, taste (Rasa • రసము). Manifests as blood plasma, synovial fluid, saliva, digestive juices, and cellular hydration.

Earth / Structure

Pṛthvī (पृथिवी • పృథ్వి)

Solidity, smell (Gandha • గంధము). Manifests as bones, teeth, muscles, tendons, hair, cartilage, and cellular density.

⚖️ 2. The Tri-Doṣa Matrix (त्रिदोष सिद्धान्त • త్రిదోష సిద్ధాంతము)

Health (Svāsthya • స్వాస్థ్యము) is the dynamic equilibrium of the three biological humors governing all physiological and psychological processes in the human organism.

💨

Vāta (वात • వాతము)

Ether (Ākāśa) + Air (Vāyu)

The principle of kinetic movement. Governs breathing, nerve impulses, cardiac contractions, joint mobility, and sensory perception.

Qualities (Guṇas): Dry, light, cold, rough, subtle, mobile.
Primary Seat: Colon (Pakvāśaya • పక్వాశయము), pelvis, ears, bones.
🔥

Pitta (पित्त • పిత్తము)

Fire (Tejas) + Water (Āpas)

The principle of metabolic transformation. Governs cellular digestion, thermal regulation, biochemical enzymes, liver metabolism, and intellect.

Qualities (Guṇas): Hot, sharp, light, oily, penetrating, spreading.
Primary Seat: Small intestine (Grahaṇī • గ్రహణి), stomach, blood, liver.
💧

Kapha (कफ • కఫము)

Water (Jala) + Earth (Pṛthvī)

The principle of cohesion, stability, and structure. Governs physical lubrication, tissue density, immunity (Ojas), hydration, and emotional forgiveness.

Qualities (Guṇas): Heavy, cold, soft, unctuous, sweet, static.
Primary Seat: Chest (Uras • ఉరస్సు), throat, joints, lymph.

🔥 5. Caturvidha Agni & Āma (चतुर्विधाग्निः • చతుర్విధ అగ్నులు & ఆమము)

"रोगाः सर्वेऽपि मन्देऽग्नौ" — All physical diseases originate from impaired digestive fire (Agni) leading to toxic metabolic waste (Āma).

Balanced Fire (Tridoṣa Equilibrium)

1. Samāgni (समाग्नि • సమాగ్ని)

Digestion is punctual, comfortable, without gas, burning, or heaviness. Generates pure Ojas (vital immunity).

Irregular Fire (Vāta Dominated)

2. Viṣamāgni (विषमाग्नि • విషమాగ్ని)

Erratic appetite, bloating, gas, constipation. Digestion alternates between fast and incomplete. Treat with warm hing, ginger, ghee.

Intense Hyper Fire (Pitta Dominated)

3. Tīkṣṇāgni (तीक्ष्णाग्नि • తీక్ష్ణాగ్ని)

Voracious burning hunger, acid reflux, heartburn, loose stools. Treat with cooling coriander, fennel, amla, and ghee.

Sluggish Hypo Fire (Kapha Dominated)

4. Mandāgni (मन्दाग्नि • మందాగ్ని)

Extremely slow metabolism, post-meal stupor, coated tongue, excessive mucus. Treat with trikatu (ginger, black pepper, pippali), fasting.

👅 6. Ṣaḍ-Rasa (षड्रस • షడ్రసాలు — Six Pharmacodynamic Tastes)

In Ayurveda, food is medicine. Every taste contains specific primary elements that immediately increase or balance the three biological humors.

Earth + Water

1. Madhura (Sweet • मधुर • మధుర రసము)

Nourishing, grounding, tissue building. Balances Vata & Pitta, increases Kapha. E.g. Ghee, rice, milk, dates.

Earth + Fire

2. Amla (Sour • अम्ल • ఆమ్ల రసము)

Stimulates digestive fire, awakens appetite. Balances Vata, increases Pitta & Kapha. E.g. Lemon, amla, yogurt.

Water + Fire

3. Lavaṇa (Salty • लवण • లవణ రసము)

Maintains electrolyte balance, softens tissues. Balances Vata, increases Pitta & Kapha. E.g. Saindhava (Rock salt).

Fire + Air

4. Kaṭu (Pungent • कटु • కటు రసము)

Clears sinuses, stimulates circulation. Balances Kapha, increases Pitta & Vata. E.g. Ginger, black pepper, pippali.

Air + Ether

5. Tikta (Bitter • तिक्त • తిక్త రసము)

Deeply detoxifying, cools blood, liver cleanser. Balances Pitta & Kapha, increases Vata. E.g. Neem, turmeric, leafy greens.

Air + Earth

6. Kaṣāya (Astringent • कषाय • కషాయ రసము)

Heals wounds, dries excess moisture, tones membranes. Balances Pitta & Kapha, increases Vata. E.g. Triphala, pomegranate, green tea.

🧬 9. Sapta Dhātus (सप्तधातवः • సప్తధాతువులు — The Seven Body Tissues)

The sequential metabolic transformation from consumed food into structural tissues and supreme vitality (Ojas • ఓజస్సు).

1. Rasa (रस • రసము) Plasma & Lymph
2. Rakta (रक्त • రక్తము) Blood & Oxygen
3. Māṁsa (मांस • మాంసము) Muscle & Strength
4. Medas (मेदस् • మేదస్సు) Adipose & Lubrication
5. Asthi (अस्थि • అస్థి) Bones & Skeleton
6. Majjā (मज्जा • మజ్జ) Marrow & Nerves
7. Śukra (शुक्र • శుక్రము) Reproductive Vitality

🏛️ 10. Aṣṭāṅga Āyurveda (अष्टाङ्ग आयुर्वेदः • అష్టాంగ ఆయుర్వేదము — 8 Classical Clinical Branches)

The eight medical specializations formalized in Charaka and Sushruta Samhitas:

Internal Medicine

1. Kāyacikitsā (कायचिकित्सा • కాయచికిత్స)

Diagnosis and treatment of general systemic diseases, metabolic disorders, fevers, and digestion.

Pediatrics & Obstetrics

2. Kaumārabhṛtya (कौमारभृत्य • కౌమారభృత్యము)

Care of infants, children, maternal health, prenatal nutrition, and pediatric ailments.

Psychiatry & Mind

3. Grahacikitsā (ग्रहचिकित्सा • గ్రహచికిత్స)

Treating psychosomatic disorders, emotional instability, neuroses, and spiritual disturbances.

ENT & Ophthalmology

4. Śālākyatantra (शालाक्यतन्त्र • శాలాక్యతంత్రము)

Diseases of eyes, ears, nose, throat, head, and teeth (supraclavicular organs).

Surgery & Anatomy

5. Śalyatantra (शल्यतन्त्र • శల్యతంత్రము — Suśruta)

Pioneering surgical procedures, plastic rhinoplasty, foreign body extraction, wound care, and fractures.

Toxicology

6. Agadatantra (अगदतन्त्र • అగదతంత్రము)

Antidotes for poisons, insect/snake bites, heavy metal toxicity, and environmental pollutants.

Rejuvenation & Geriatrics

7. Rasāyana (रसायनतन्त्र • రసాయన తంత్రము)

Longevity therapy, cellular rejuvenation, memory enhancement, and supreme immune resistance (Ojas).

Reproductive Vitality

8. Vājīkaraṇa (वाजीकरणतन्त्र • వాజీకరణ తంత్రము)

Reproductive science, fertility enhancement, aphrodisiacs, and vitality preservation.
