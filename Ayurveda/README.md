## 🌟 Comprehensive 10-Pillar System

1. **Pañca Mahābhūta (पञ्चमहाभूतानि • పంచమహాభూతాలు)**:
   - Ākāśa (Ether • ఆకాశము), Vāyu (Air • వాయువు), Agni (Fire • తేజస్సు), Jala (Water • జలము), and Pṛthvī (Earth • పృథ్వి).
2. **Tri-Doṣa Matrix (त्रिदोष • త్రిదోషాలు)**:
   - **Vāta (వాతము)** = Air + Ether (Movement, breath, nervous signals).
   - **Pitta (పిత్తము)** = Fire + Water (Metabolic transformation, enzymes, thermoregulation).
   - **Kapha (కఫము)** = Earth + Water (Structure, cohesion, immunity, lubrication).
3. **Prakṛti Diagnostic Assessment (ప్రకృతి పరీక్ష)**:
   - 8-dimension clinical questionnaire computing lifelong constitutional dosha percentages.
4. **Tri-Guṇas & Mānasa Prakṛti (త్రిగుణాలు — Psychic Energetics)**:
   - **Sattva (సత్త్వము)**: Harmony, clarity, compassion.
   - **Rajas (రజస్సు)**: Dynamic passion, agitation, ambition.
   - **Tamas (తమస్సు)**: Heaviness, inertia, lethargy.
5. **Caturvidha Agni & Āma (చతుర్విధ అగ్నులు & ఆమము)**:
   - Samāgni (balanced), Viṣamāgni (Vata irregular), Tīkṣṇāgni (Pitta hyper), Mandāgni (Kapha sluggish).
   - Causes of metabolic endotoxins (Āma) and classical purification (*Dīpana-Pācana*).
6. **Ṣaḍ-Rasa Pharmacodynamics (షడ్రసాలు)**:
   - Madhura (Sweet), Amla (Sour), Lavaṇa (Salty), Kaṭu (Pungent), Tikta (Bitter), and Kaṣāya (Astringent).
7. **Dinacaryā (దినచర్య — Circadian Clock)**:
   - Real-time 24-hour doshic period indicator reflecting the user's current local hour.
8. **Ṛtu-caryā (ఋతుచర్య — Seasonal Biorhythms)**:
   - Protocols for Śiśira, Vasanta, Grīṣma, Varṣā, Śarad, and Hemanta, tracking *Sañcaya* (accumulation), *Prakopa* (aggravation), and *Praśamana* (pacification).
9. **Sapta Dhātus (సప్తధాతువులు — Seven Bodily Tissues)**:
   - Sequential transformation: Rasa (Plasma) $\rightarrow$ Rakta (Blood) $\rightarrow$ Māṁsa (Muscle) $\rightarrow$ Medas (Fat) $\rightarrow$ Asthi (Bone) $\rightarrow$ Majjā (Marrow) $\rightarrow$ Śukra (Reproductive vitality) $\rightarrow$ **Ojas (Immune radiance)**.
10. **Aṣṭāṅga Āyurveda (అష్టాంగ ఆయుర్వేదము — 8 Classical Clinical Branches)**:
    - Kāyacikitsā, Kaumārabhṛtya, Grahacikitsā, Śālākyatantra, Śalyatantra (Suśruta), Agadatantra, Rasāyana, Vājīkaraṇa.
